# Free rigged characters: survey, licences, downloads and SOMA-23 mapping

Survey date: 2026-10-27. Machine-readable record: `config/character-sources.json` (generated by
`scripts/characters/build_character_sources.py`; file hashes, licence-text hashes and the proposed joint maps).
Binaries live in the gitignored `.runtime/downloads/characters/<source>/`, each with `LICENSE.txt` and `SOURCE.txt`.
Rig reports (joint names, hierarchy, rest/bind positions, vertex counts) are in `.runtime/characters/reports/*.json`,
produced by `scripts/characters/inspect_rig.py` from a small uv environment at `.runtime/characters/venv`
(CPython 3.12.13 from `.runtime/python`, numpy 2.5.4, pygltflib 1.16.5, trimesh 5.2.0). No GPU, Docker, Blender or
Isaac was used.

## Target skeleton

SOMA-23 as in `vendor/ProtoMotions/protomotions/data/assets/mjcf/soma23_humanoid.xml`: 23 bodies, Z-up, metres, the
rest pose faces -Y, right side at -X. Rest offsets from the MJCF give these reference segment lengths (metres):
upper arm 0.2874, forearm 0.2713, thigh 0.4337, shin 0.4226, foot to toe 0.1422, hips to head joint 0.599,
shoulder (Arm to Arm) 0.3296; the Head joint sits 0.599 m above the Hips.

## Proposed joint maps (SOMA-23 body -> rig joint)

Mixamo-style naming is close to SOMA but not identical: Mixamo `Spine/Spine1/Spine2` correspond to SOMA
`Spine1/Spine2/Chest`, Mixamo `UpLeg/Leg` to SOMA `Leg/Shin`, and Mixamo has one `Neck` (SOMA `Neck2` unmapped).
Full tables are in `config/character-sources.json`; condensed:

| Hips | Hips | pelvis | Hips | Bip01 Pelvis | hips | Skeleton_torso_joint_1 / torso_joint_1 |

Kenney Blocky: rigid parts only (Hips=root, Chest=torso, Head=head, Arm=arm-left/right, Leg=leg-left/right); 16 SOMA
bodies have no counterpart. The SOMA-X USDA joints are paths (`Root/Hips/Spine1/...`); the leaf names are the SOMA-23
names, so the map is the identity after stripping the path (regex recorded in the config). 100Avatars joints carry a
per-file prefix (`vis_char_001_fbx:mixamorig:`), also handled by a recorded regex; the VRM `humanoid.humanBones` block
offers a second, standard name map (hips, spine, chest, upperChest, neck, head, ...).

Topology caveats: Rocketbox's Biped export parents the clavicles to `Bip01 Neck` and the thighs to `Bip01 Spine`;
KayKit and the Cesium rigs have no clavicles, so SOMA shoulder motion must be folded into the arm joint; every rig
except SOMA-X lacks the second neck joint.

Every mapped joint name was checked against the parsed reports (`mapping-check.json`: no missing joints).

## Recommended first character: SOMA-X template rig

`SOMA_template_rig.usda` (Hugging Face `nvidia/SOMA-X`, revision `f81a0179`, Apache-2.0):

- Licence: Apache-2.0 with an explicit "ready for commercial use" statement; redistribution with the LICENSE is
  allowed; no attribution beyond the notice; no account gating.
- Skeleton: identical body names and offsets to the physics skeleton; the 23 physical bodies are a subset of its 110
  joints, so each body pose can be written straight into the UsdSkel animation; fingers/eyes/jaw can stay at rest.
- Format: UsdSkel text USD, which Isaac Sim loads natively (no glTF/FBX conversion), with skin weights present.
- Mesh: 136,739 vertices across the hi-res body and eye meshes (a mid-res body mesh `c_skin_mid` is also inside the
  file; use it for real-time playback if the hi-res one is slow).
- Drawback: no textures (neutral grey body) and a neutral, anonymous look; `example_animation.usd` is a ready test
  clip.

Runner-up for a textured, realistic human: Microsoft Rocketbox (MIT, 115 avatars, 4k vertices, colour/normal/specular
textures, segment lengths within 1 cm of SOMA for the arms and 3 cm for the legs). It needs an FBX to USD/glTF
conversion step (Blender or the FBX SDK) and a Biped-to-SOMA retarget, so it comes after the SOMA-X rig. Quaternius
Superhero (CC0, glTF, PBR textures) is the best stylised option that loads without conversion.

## Prepared characters and how they are driven

`config/characters.json` lists the characters the studio prepares; `scripts/fetch_characters.py` verifies each rig
file's hash against this survey's record, copies the licence text next to it and runs
`scripts/prepare_character.py` on the CPU inside the pinned image. The result, `.runtime/characters/<id>/`, holds
`rig.npz` (joint names and hierarchy, bind and inverse bind matrices, the chosen skinned meshes with weights, uvs and
triangulated faces), the base colour textures as PNG and `character.json` (name, licence, source, joint map, counts).
Nothing binary is committed.

| id | Rig | Mesh used | Vertices | Joints | Bodies mapped |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `soma_x` | SOMA-X template rig (UsdSkel) | `c_skin_mid` | 18,056 | 110 | 23 of 23 |
| `superhero_male` / `superhero_female` | Quaternius Universal Base Characters (glTF) | body, eyes, eyebrows | 8,483 | 65 | 22 of 23 (no Neck2) |
| `kaykit_knight` | KayKit Adventurers (GLB) | body | about 7k | 41 | 18 of 23 |
| `cesium_man` | Khronos glTF sample (GLB) | body | 3,273 | 19 | 19 of 23 |

The live worker (`scripts/studio_walk/character_rig.py`) loads that folder, converts the rig to metres and Z-up,
aligns its bind pose to the physics body's MJCF rest pose (each mapped joint is rotated so its bone points along the
matching body's bone; unmapped joints ride on their nearest mapped ancestor; the hips snap to the body's hips) and,
after every control step, skins every mesh on the GPU from the 23 body poses and writes the points and smooth
normals into USD mesh prims under `/World/Character`, with a UsdPreviewSurface material (textured when the rig has
uvs and a base colour texture). The capsule body is made invisible. The character therefore follows the physical
body exactly, in physical and kinematic sessions alike; a rig with other limb lengths keeps its own proportions.

## Manual-only path: Mixamo

Not shipped. A user may sign in at mixamo.com with their own Adobe ID, download a character (FBX Binary, T-pose, with
skin) into `.runtime/downloads/characters/mixamo/<name>/` with a `SOURCE.txt`, and the studio can import it locally
with the Mixamo joint map above. The files must never be committed or redistributed with the studio.

## Blockers and unverified items

- The live Adobe Mixamo FAQ returned HTTP 403 to this host; the quoted terms come from the 2026-01-05 Wayback snapshot
  of the 2021 page and a 2022 community re-post. Verify against the live page before relying on them.
- kenney.nl's download links refuse scripted fetches; the Animated Characters and Mini Characters packs remain
  unfetched (CC0 per their pages).
- Blender Studio characters are `.blend`-only; no Blender on this host, so no parse or conversion was done.
- The Rocketbox FBX positions come from a minimal FBX reader (translation, rotation, pre-rotation only); exact bind
  matrices and pivots were not evaluated. Only one of 115 avatars was fetched; the full repository is about 4 GB.
- SOMA-X's `correctives_model.pt` (63 MB, pose correctives) and the hand model were not fetched; they are not needed
  for driving the body from the physics skeleton.
